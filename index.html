ENABLE INDIA • EST. 1999 Non-Profit Organisation • Economic Independence & Inclusion

ABOUT US

EnAble India is a non-profit organisation established in 1999, working towards promoting economic independence, inclusion and dignity for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs).

We believe that persons with disabilities are valuable contributors to society and change agents who can positively influence communities, the economy and the nation when provided with equal opportunities and an inclusive environment. EnAble India is working to build a sustainable livelihood ecosystem that enables millions of persons with disabilities to achieve economic independence and lead lives of dignity.

RURAL LIVELIHOOD MISSION • EST. 2018

ABOUT GARVSE PROGRAMME

EnAble India's GarvSe Rural Livelihood Mission was initiated in 2018 to serve as one-stop access points for PwDs to obtain information, guidance and support to access various disability-related services, entitlements and livelihood opportunities. The programme also focuses on promoting rural entrepreneurship among PwDs by facilitating self-employment opportunities, entrepreneurship development and the creation of sustainable business plans. Today, the programme has its presence across 10 States and 2 Union Territories, working through a network of 45 GarvSe Centre partners, with a mission to create sustainable livelihood opportunities for persons with disabilities in rural India.

KERALA NETWORK • 8 DISTRICTS

GarvSe Programme in Kerala

In Kerala, EnAble India is currently working with four GarvSe partners – Akkara Foundation, Samadrushti, Cottolengo Vocational & Rehabilitation Centre, NIDS – covering eight districts.

As part of our continued efforts to strengthen the disability inclusion ecosystem in the state, EnAble India, in collaboration with its GarvSe partner, Akkara Foundation, is planning to organise a mega event titled “Jagruthi Mela” – a Day-long Mass Sensitisation and Awareness Initiative for Persons with Disabilities.

Jagruthi Mela is envisioned as a common platform that brings together government departments, service providers, financial institutions, healthcare professionals, educational institutions, disability organisations, corporates and other key stakeholders working for the welfare and empowerment of persons with disabilities. The primary objective is to bridge the information and accessibility gaps faced by PwDs and their families by creating awareness on various aspects of PwDs life.

INITIATIVE OBJECTIVES

Through this initiative, we aim to:

  • 01 Create awareness among PwDs, their families and communities about available government schemes, services and entitlements.
  • 02 Bring various service providers and institutions onto a common platform to facilitate direct access to information and services.
  • 03 Enable PwDs to understand and access support systems through a life-cycle approach, from early intervention and education to employment, entrepreneurship and independent living.
  • 04 Promote an inclusive, accessible environment where persons with disabilities can participate fully and exercise their rights.
